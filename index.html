House Rules

  • 1
    Four on, four offWhen courts are full and players are waiting, games go to 11 and the whole court rotates.
  • 2
    Winners stay — max twoWin twice in a row and you rotate out anyway. Everybody plays everybody.
  • 3
    Call the score, loudFull score before every serve: yours, theirs, and the server number. No silent serves.
  • 4
    The kitchen is lavaNo volleys from the no-volley zone. Toe on the line counts — we don't argue about it twice.
  • 5
    Line calls belong to the receiving sideTheir call stands. Too close to call? It's in. Replay beats debate.
  • 6
    New faces get a warm welcomeFirst-timer in your game? Walk them through the rotation and give them one mulligan serve.
